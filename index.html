Chow Hall

Dinner, sorted for the whole week.

Chow Hall plans your family's meals, builds the grocery list for you and keeps everyone in the house on the same page, with a little help from a raccoon chef.

  • Plan your whole week in one tap
  • Chow Haul: a grocery list sorted by aisle
  • Shared lists and a potluck planner
  • Levels and badges as you cook

About the studio

Why a raccoon?

Raccoons are clever, curious and very good at finding food. That felt about right for a studio whose first app helps you figure out what’s for dinner. RingTailApps is independently owned and run, and every app is designed, built and supported in-house.
